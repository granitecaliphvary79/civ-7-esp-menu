## 🧩 About

**Civilization 7 Companion** is a curated list of Civilization 7 open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, map markers, resource viewer
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — city planner notes, unit tracker, build-queue helper
- **Research Notes** — tech tree layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Map Overlay — visualize tiles, borders, and resources
- Resource Viewer — track strategic and luxury resources at a glance
- Turn Notes — record and review empire plans
- Playback Speed — adjust camera and pacing for study

### Wallhack
- City Planner — organize districts, buildings, and specialists
- Unit Tracker — keep an eye on movement and positions
- Trade Notes — log deals and diplomatic offers

### Aimbot
- Tech Tree Notes — segment study across ages
- Government Planner — compare policy cards side by side
- Wonder Tracker — chain build plans in the editor
